# Afterimage

[Watch or download the finished edit](https://github.com/7etsuo/imagine-1.5/raw/refs/heads/main/afterimage_anvil_v2.mp4).

A 28.8-second cyberpunk portrait edit, finished in DaVinci Resolve with 2.5D depth movement, a custom violet and pink grade, animated glow and fine grain. The main eye reveal meets the percussion entrance around 9.6 seconds.

Music by LORN, [ANVIL](https://lorn.bandcamp.com/track/anvil).

The seven original clips are the `grok-*.mp4` files in this repository. The finished edit is `afterimage_anvil_v2.mp4`, at 1152 x 1752 and 24 fps.
