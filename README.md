# Seven Shells

[Watch or download the edit](https://github.com/7etsuo/imagine-1.5/raw/refs/heads/main/seven_shells.mp4).

A 62 second portrait film cut from the seven Grok Imagine clips in this repository, finished in DaVinci Resolve. One ghost, seven bodies: a sleeping cyborg switches shells on every taiko pulse, wakes when the choir enters, and the shells overlap and converge into one gaze before she turns into the light.

Each shot is a live Fusion composition: 2x upscaled frames, per-frame depth maps and character mattes, an inpainted background plate, depth parallax pinned to her eyes so the camera orbits her gaze, depth wipes between shells, screen-blended ghost layers, and a glow keyed to the drum hits. 1536 x 2336, 24 fps.

Music: Kenji Kawai, "Making of Cyborg" (Ghost in the Shell, 1995). Personal project.

The seven original clips are the `grok-*.mp4` files. The edit is `seven_shells.mp4`.
